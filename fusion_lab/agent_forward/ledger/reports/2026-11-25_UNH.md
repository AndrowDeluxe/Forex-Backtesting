## 8. Data Quality and Caveats

1. **Q1 2024 net income is -$1,409M, with EPS of -$1.53.** The magnitude matches the roughly $1.4B positive figure I would expect for that quarter, so this may be a sign-convention or tagging error. Verify against the original filing before using the historical series.
2. **TTM figures may not be true trailing-twelve-month values.** TTM net income ($15.88B) almost exactly equals the sum of the three 2026 quarters ($15.87B), and TTM EPS ($17.52) is close to the 9-month sum ($17.50). The income statement table also has no Q4 2024 or Q4 2025 rows, so TTM cannot be rebuilt from the quarterly data. Treat trailing multiples as approximate.
3. **PEG of 11.55 conflicts with the forward EPS.** Forward EPS of $23.47 implies about 34% growth over TTM EPS of $17.52, which would give a PEG near 0.6. The 11.55 value likely reflects a different growth basis.
4. **The medical cost ratio, membership, and segment data are missing.** These are the core drivers for a managed-care company and are required for a full assessment.
5. **Implied price (~$346)** is derived from P/B and P/E inputs and should be confirmed with a live quote.
6. **Cash-flow periods** are cumulative YTD and were converted to quarters by subtraction, which may compound rounding differences.
