Free cash flow fell sharply in the latest half-year because capital spending grew faster than operating cash flow. Insider selling is heavy and there are no open-market insider purchases in the data. Several TTM figures do not reconcile with the quarterly data, so they should be verified before anyone relies on them.

**Fundamental stance: Neutral to cautiously constructive.** The business is sound. The price already reflects a good deal of that quality.

## 7. Valuation Assessment

- **P/E (TTM) of 50x** is very high for a retailer with a 2.8% net margin. It depends on the TTM earnings base, which has data issues (Section 8).
- **Forward P/E of 41x** implies forward EPS of $3.23, about 23% above TTM EPS of $2.62. Delivering this would require sustained earnings growth well above the current pace.
- **PEG of 2.18** indicates the price is not cheap relative to expected growth.
- **Price/book of 10.4x** is high. Returns depend on sustained high ROE (20.6%).
- **The dividend yield of 0.75%** is low. Shareholder return depends largely on share price appreciation.

**Implication:** The quality and stability of the business support a premium, but the current multiple leaves little margin of safety against an earnings miss or multiple compression.

## 8. Data Quality Flags (verify before relying on TTM metrics)

1. **The January-2026 quarter is missing** from the quarterly income statement and cash flow tables, though it appears in the balance sheet.
2. **TTM figures do not reconcile with the available quarters.** Summing Q3 FY26 through Q2 FY27 gives revenue of about $539.6B, which would imply the missing Q4 FY26 was about $205.6B. That is well above every other quarter in the table. Net income would imply about $3.0B for Q4 (EPS about $0.38), which is unusually low. Either the fundamentals TTM figures use a different basis, or Q4 FY26 contains a large one-time charge or a data-provider mismatch.
3. **Current ratio differs:** the fundamentals report shows 0.81, while the balance sheet gives about 0.77.
4. **The October-2026 quarter (fiscal Q3 FY27) is absent** from the data, which is filtered to filings on or before 2026-11-25. If that quarter has already been reported, the figures here are stale.
5. **Total debt is not tagged** in the balance sheet data, so leverage can only be checked against the reported debt-to-equity ratio.

## 9. Trader Implications

**Constructive factors**
- Steady revenue growth of about 6–7%.
- Operating income accelerating, with H1 FY27 up 17%.
- Gross margin improving modestly.
- Low beta (0.53) and moderate leverage.
- Rising equity and cash.

**Concerns**
- Valuation is stretched: forward P/E of 41x, P/B of 10.4x, and price well above the moving averages.
- Free cash flow down about 20% in H1 FY27 because capex is growing faster than operating cash flow.
- Net income lagging operating income.
- Heavy insider and major-holder selling, with no insider buying.
- Unresolved data inconsistencies in the TTM figures.

**Suggested approach**
- Do not chase at elevated levels. Price is extended well above the 50-day and 200-day averages.
- A pullback toward the $117–119 moving-average zone would bring valuation closer to a level where the fundamentals offer more support, if the thesis holds.
- Re-assess after the October-2026 quarter is confirmed and the January-2026 quarter is reconciled.
- Key items to watch: whether capex translates into operating margin gains, whether FCF stabilizes, and whether net income catches up to operating income.

This is a fundamental read only. It is not a price target or a trade instruction.
