The gap is likely driven by non-operating items such as equity-investment fair-value changes, but the dataset does not break this out. Treat GAAP EPS as a weaker signal than operating income.

## 7. Risks and Catalysts

**Risks**
- **Valuation compression:** At 41x forward earnings and a 0.6% FCF yield, a growth shortfall could produce a sharp de-rating.
- **Capex intensity:** Rising capex against slower cash growth may weigh on FCF through FY27 if the spending does not generate returns.
- **Earnings quality:** GAAP net income depends heavily on non-operating items, which makes EPS comparisons unreliable.
- **Working-capital pressure:** Current liabilities have grown faster than current assets.
- **Insider selling:** Concentrated sales by the Walton trust could resume.
- **Data inconsistencies:** TTM figures do not reconcile with the disclosed quarters (see Section 3).

**Supportive factors**
- Operating income momentum (Q2 FY27 +28.8%).
- Revenue growth in the 5–7% range, which is strong for a company of this size.
- Low beta (0.53) and moderate leverage.
- Q2 FCF recovery and rising equity.

## 9. Bottom Line for Traders

- **Operating fundamentals are improving.** Revenue growth of 5–7% and the Q2 FY27 operating margin expansion are real positives.
- **Valuation prices in continued strength.** At about $133, the stock trades at 41x forward earnings with a near-zero FCF yield. Upside requires sustained margin gains; a single soft quarter could pressure the multiple.
- **Cash-flow quality needs monitoring.** The next checkpoints are whether capex growth slows and whether operating cash flow keeps pace with revenue.
- **Insider data does not give a clear directional signal.** The absence of insider buying and the pause in Walton trust sales are both neutral-to-mildly-positive observations, while the executive sales are routine.
- **Actionable monitoring points:**
  - Confirm the fiscal Q3 FY27 results and whether operating margin holds above about 4.5%.
  - Watch whether H2 FCF recovers and whether capex growth moderates below revenue growth.
  - Track any resumption of Walton trust sales.
  - Treat price levels near the 52-week high ($135) with caution given the valuation premium; the 50- and 200-day averages (about $118–119) represent the nearest reference support.
